How Verification Works

Three steps. Zero trust required. Mathematical proof only.

1

Record Created

A vehicle event — intake scan, custody transfer, condition report — occurs at a Cox Automotive facility. The data is captured and digitally signed by the handling agent.

2

Sealed on the Ledger

The signed record is submitted to the Cox Automotive Ledger — a private, Proof-of-Authority enterprise chain. It's hashed, timestamped, and sealed into a block by an authorized validator node.

3

Verify Here

Anyone with a Certificate ID or VIN can verify the record's authenticity. The hallmark stamp confirms the data hasn't been altered since it was sealed — no trust required, just math.
